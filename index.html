Frequently Asked Questions ❓

What is QuizMaster?

QuizMaster is a fun and educational quiz platform for students and teachers to learn, compete, and track performance.

How do I register?

Simply click on the “Register” button in the navigation bar and fill out your details to create an account.

Can I create my own quizzes?

Yes! Admin users have full control to create, edit, and manage quizzes directly from the admin dashboard.

Is QuizMaster free to use?

Absolutely! QuizMaster is completely free for both students and teachers.

How are results calculated?

Scores are automatically computed based on your answers, and detailed reports are available instantly after submission.
